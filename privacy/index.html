Privacy Policy: I Forgot the Cards

Effective date: 14 October 2026
Last updated: 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ashlo Studios (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the mobile app I Forgot the Cards (the "App"). We have designed the App to minimise the collection of personal information. This policy explains what information is stored on your device and what information may be processed by third-party services when you make purchases.

1. The short version

  • The App does not require an account, login, or sign-up.
  • Your game data, settings, preferences, and user-created content are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not operate servers that store your game data or user-created content.
  • We do not use advertising networks, analytics services, or cross-app tracking.
  • If you make an in-app purchase, Apple and our subscription-management provider, RevenueCat, may process information necessary to process, verify, and manage your purchase.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores certain information locally on your device, including settings, game progress, preferences, player names, and content you create within the App, such as custom cards, rules, and titles.

This information is stored using the device's local storage and is not uploaded to or accessible by us. We do not have access to your locally stored game data or user-created content.

Deleting the App will generally remove the locally stored App data from your device. You may also be able to clear certain App data through the App's settings, where available.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not directly collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, precise or approximate location, or information about your locally stored game data or user-created content. The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, or contacts, because it does not use those features.

The App does not use advertising networks, analytics services, or cross-app tracking technologies.

If you make an in-app purchase, information necessary to process or manage that purchase may be processed by Apple and RevenueCat as described in Section 4.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

If you purchase a subscription or other in-app purchase, the transaction is processed through Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details or billing address.

To manage subscription status and restore purchases, we use RevenueCat, Inc., a third-party subscription-management service. RevenueCat may receive purchase-related information, such as an App Store purchase receipt and an app-specific identifier, to determine whether a subscription is active and to provide subscription-management functionality.

The app-specific identifier used for subscription management does not directly identify you to us by name or contact information. However, information processed by Apple or RevenueCat may be subject to their respective privacy policies and may be treated as personal information or personal data under applicable law.

Apple's privacy policy is available at: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/

Managing or cancelling an auto-renewing subscription is done through your Apple ID subscription settings. Refund requests are handled by Apple in accordance with Apple's applicable policies.

5. Children

The App is intended for adults who meet the applicable legal drinking age and is not directed to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us using the details in Section 8.

6. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your game data or user-created content on our servers, we generally do not have access to information such as your player names, custom cards, custom rules, titles, game progress, or settings.

You remain in control of information stored locally by the App. Depending on the App's available features, you may be able to clear certain local data through the App's settings. Deleting the App will generally remove its locally stored data from your device.

Purchase and subscription information is processed by Apple and, where applicable, RevenueCat. Requests relating to Apple purchases, including refunds, should generally be directed to Apple.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined under applicable law.

Depending on where you live, you may have privacy rights under applicable law, including rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of personal information. If you have a privacy question or believe we hold personal information about you, you can contact us using the details in Section 8. We will respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including when we introduce new features or change how the App or third-party services process information.

We will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy. Where required by applicable law, we will provide additional notice of material changes.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, or if you wish to make a privacy-related request, please contact us:
